About KETHIVO

Practical Digital Guidance Built for Indian Farmers

KETHIVO brings weather intelligence, crop planning, irrigation scheduling estimates, AI leaf screening, fertilizer guidance, and market trends into one clean, accessible workspace—without costly field hardware.

Core Capabilities

Everything Needed for Informed Farming Decisions

💧

Smart Irrigation Advisory

Software-based irrigation estimates combining crop stage, soil type, field condition, and live rainfall forecasts.

🌱

Crop Recommendation

Rule-based regional crop matching tailored to your state, district, water availability, and season.

⛅

Live Weather Forecast

Real-time temperature, humidity, wind, rainfall, and 7-day forecasts with farming advisories.

🔬

AI Disease Detection

Upload crop leaf photos for AI-assisted disease screening, symptoms, and preventive actions.

🧪

Fertilizer Advisory

Stage-wise nutrient planning with optional NPK/pH soil test inputs and soil health guidance.

📈

Market Prices & Trends

Compare mandi prices across regions and visualize historical trends using interactive charts.
